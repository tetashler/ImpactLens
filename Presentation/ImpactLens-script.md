## Slide 3: The Pressing Problem `[0:40 – 1:10]`

Planning tools can map where people may go, but they can't tell us how people react.



- Besides, Surveys and focus groups take months, late discovery gaps show up mid-project, which pushes timelines back and budgets up. And worse, concerns only surface after launch, turning feedback into opposition.

---

## Slide 4: Our Solution `[1:10 – 1:45]`

Our solution is ImpactLens. It simulates **AI citizens** built on real Singapore demographic data, so you can gauge community impact before anything is built.



- Firstly, **data-grounded AI citizens** are built from real SingStat demographic data.
- Secondly, A single run shows sentiment, concerns, usage and ripple effects all broken down by demographic.
- Thirdly, a **knowledge graph**, which gives you a visual map of impact across demographics.

---

## Slide 5: Target User Group `[1:45 – 2:00]`

Our users are urban planners and transport authorities, government policymakers, and agency policymakers. All three can use ImpactLens to test their options *before* decisions are made.

---

## Slide 6: Key Features `[2:00 – 2:35]`

We built ImpactLens to make social impact forecasting simple, transparent and actionable.

**In-depth reports** show you each demographic's concerns and what to fix first. The **knowledge graph** shows how people, places and impacts connect.

Most importantly, **a human must decide**. The user gets to approve or deny certain assumptions from being made during simulation.

Now let me show you how it works.

---

## Slide 7: Demo video `[2:35 – 3:35]`
Now let me take you through the workflow. To mark the location of the infrastructure you would like to simulate before building. Click 'Plot Tools' and add a point on the map. You can then either type in your scenario, or use our speech to text generator.

Clicking explore does not immediately start a simulation. It generates assumptions that will be made during the simulation, allowing you to either accept or not allow the assumption.

After completing the assumption list, you can click on the knowledge graph tab to see AI citizens interact in real time. This video is actually sped up to save time.

Once simulation finishes, AI-generated summary and detailed AI report is automatically generated.

---

## Slide 8: Knowledge Graph `[3:35 – 3:55]`

Here's the knowledge graph after a simulation. It shows the interactions and relationships between the AI citizens. If you click on any node, you get a summary of that node's interactions. This lets planners see *who* is affected and *why*.

---

## Slide 9: AI Summary and Detailed Report `[3:55 – 4:25]`

When the simulation finishes, ImpactLens writes an **AI summary** of the knowledge graph. It also generates a **detailed report** with six sections:

- Overall assessment
- Calculated impacts
- Stakeholder perspectives
- Agreements
- How the discussion developed
- Open questions and next steps

Planners get something they can act on right away.

---

## Slide 10: Responsible AI `[4:25 – 5:00]`

We take responsible AI seriously, because this tool supports public decisions. It rests on four principles:

- **Grounded in real data.** We use real census, housing and transport data.
- **Transparent by design.** We make assumptions and risks visible.
- **An exploratory tool.** It supports fast exploration, not final judgement.
- **Privacy protection.** We use providers with zero data retention.

AI informs decisions, and people remain accountable.
